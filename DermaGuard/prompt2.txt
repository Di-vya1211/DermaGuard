Build class ImageQualityAgent with:
- compute_metrics(bgr_image) returning a dict: blur (variance of Laplacian on grayscale), brightness (mean gray 0-255), contrast (std of gray), glare_ratio (fraction of pixels with HSV V>240 and S<40), dark_corner_ratio (fraction of dark pixels, gray<30, in the four corner patches, each 15% of width/height), occlusion_ratio (fraction of pixels that are extreme dark gray<15 OR extreme bright gray>250 across the whole image), hair_ratio (fraction of thin dark line pixels found by a blackhat morphological filter with a 17x17 kernel then threshold 10).

Save to DermaGuard/outputs/thresholds.json including a one-line written justification per threshold and the printed distribution (min, p1, p5, median, p95, p99, max) per metric.
- assess(path_or_array) returning {"quality_score": float 0-1, "status": "GOOD"/"ACCEPTABLE"/"POOR", "reasons": [human-readable strings including the measured value and threshold], "metrics": {...}}. Rules: each failed check adds to a penalty; score = 1 - weighted penalty clipped to 0-1; status GOOD if no check failed and score>=0.8, POOR if blur below blur_poor, or two or more checks failed, or score<0.5, else ACCEPTABLE. If thresholds.json exists, load it; otherwise fit it.
- assess_folder(folder, out_csv) running assess on all jpg images in a folder (accepts ANY folder path) and saving a CSV.
